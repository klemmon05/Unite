Our Approach

Theory of change rooted in partnerships.

Unite-LA works as a connector across institutions. This concept frames strategy as sustained collaboration among K-12, higher education, employers, and civic leaders.

1. Align priorities

Build shared regional goals around student success and economic opportunity.

2. Co-design pathways

Develop programs and supports with those closest to students and communities.

3. Influence systems

Translate lessons into policy recommendations and durable practice shifts.
